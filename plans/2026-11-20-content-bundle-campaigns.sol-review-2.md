## Verdict

**Not ready.** Rev 3 adds installments to a loop whose drafting, cohesion, calendar, and review bridge are still keyed to the entire campaign. It also introduces a publishing account without deciding which identity owns the delivery, authorizes it, or occupies its calendar slot. The filed contracts would let different implementers make incompatible choices at both boundaries. M1 describes a thin product journey, but CB-03–08 still require several later-stage features and leave direct legacy entry points open. Settle the installment and delivery identities, keep publishing gated until the evidence checks can enforce J19, and correct the dependency graph before assigning the affected issues. I reviewed the 36 supplied issue snapshots against `origin/main` at `5a5f016`. This was a read-only review. Transaction headroom, deployed throttling, provider mappings, and publication were unverified.
