Propose the campaign shape

A shape is a durable plan of slots — channel × format × role — with optional title, angle, and linked ideas. The shape fixes the publishing sequence (1 → n); the calendar owns the publishing schedule (dates and times).

Presets are Corvo Labs default compositions (#63). Switching presets re-proposes the shape and keeps your edits — slots are matched by role + channel; everything else fills from the preset. Your working set is untouched.
